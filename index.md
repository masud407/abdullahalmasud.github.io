# Abdullah Al Masud

Research Fellow, Department of Radiology, Mayo Clinic

## About Me

I am a Research Fellow in the Department of Radiology at Mayo Clinic, with expertise in ultrasound imaging, elastography, Doppler signal processing, MRI, and quantitative medical image analysis. My research combines ultrasound physics, signal processing, image reconstruction, and computational analysis to develop and evaluate imaging methods for biomedical applications. My current work focuses on quantitative characterization of ultrasound signals, color Doppler twinkling, tissue and interface wave elastography, ultrasound-visible markers, and MRI-based marker visualization. I work across the imaging pipeline, from raw channel and RF/IQ data processing to feature extraction, quantitative image analysis, and experimental validation.

A central goal of my research is to translate signal-level and physical measurements into quantitative imaging techniques that improve target detection, tissue characterization, and image-guided procedures. I am particularly interested in understanding how acoustic interactions, tissue mechanical properties, and imaging-system parameters are represented in measured RF, IQ, Doppler, and image data. By connecting these underlying physical processes with quantitative signal and image features, my work aims to develop imaging biomarkers and processing methods that are more sensitive, reproducible, and clinically useful. This approach spans fundamental signal and wave analysis, imaging sequence and parameter optimization, quantitative feature extraction, and experimental validation, with the broader goal of improving diagnostic imaging and guidance for minimally invasive procedures.
