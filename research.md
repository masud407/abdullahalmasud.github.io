# Research Projects

## Quantitative Analysis of Doppler Twinkling Artifacts

Ultrasound Doppler twinkling artifacts generated by PMMA markers and kidney stones. This project investigates spectro-temporal characteristics and signal processing approaches for artifact characterization.

---

## Ultrasound Surface Wave Elastography

Development of Scholte wave and Rayleigh wave elastography methods for imaging superficial tissues.

---

## Zero Echo Time MRI of Breast Biopsy Markers

Evaluation of commercial and polymer-based breast biopsy markers using Zero Echo Time MRI.

---

## Resonance Ultrasound Spectroscopy

Estimating elastic properties of 3D printed materials and lattice structures using resonant ultrasound spectroscopy.

---

## 3D Printed Bone Tissue Scaffolds

Design and mechanical characterization of anisotropic bone tissue scaffolds with tunable stiffness.
